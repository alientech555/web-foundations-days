About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple, fast, and reliable web application designed to help you capture your thoughts on the fly. Whether you are brainstorming ideas or keeping track of daily tasks, QuickNotes ensures your notes are always just a click away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the application and navigate to the "Add a note" section.
  2. Type your thought or task into the text input field.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.

Features

  • Lightning-fast note creation
  • Clean and distraction-free interface
  • Automatic saving to local storage

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
